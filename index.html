About Us

Lakeside Medical Clinic is a neighbourhood clinic in Jumeirah Lake Towers, built around one simple idea: high-quality healthcare should be easy to reach. Our team of qualified, DHA-licensed doctors brings years of experience across general medicine, ENT, dentistry and cardiology.

We take time to listen, explain clearly and treat every patient like family — in a calm, modern and spotless setting just a short walk from your home or office.
